# Epic: Checkout Revamp

**Key goal:** shoppers complete a purchase on one page, in fewer steps than today.

**Owner:** Northwind Goods product team (fictional).

**Why now:** cart abandonment on the current three-page checkout is high on phones. The next seasonal campaign starts in six weeks.

## Scope

- One-page checkout for guests and logged-in shoppers.
- Saved addresses and saved cards for logged-in shoppers.
- Order summary with totals, shipping and tax.
- Order confirmation email for guests and logged-in shoppers.

## Success measures

- Cart-to-order conversion on phones rises compared with the current checkout.
- The number of checkout steps drops from three to one.
